Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

What exactly is OwlGarden?

Think of it as your digital brain turf. It combines note-taking, flashcards, and spaced repetition into one seamless workflow. You plant ideas, and the system ensures you don't forget them.

Is there a free tier?

Yes! The core features are free forever for personal use. We offer a Pro plan for power users who need advanced analytics, unlimited file uploads, and API access.

Do I need an internet connection?

OwlGarden works offline-first. You can write and review anywhere. Your data syncs automatically to the cloud once you're back online.

Can I export my data?

Absolutely. Your data belongs to you. You can export everything to Markdown, JSON, or PDF at any time with a single click.
